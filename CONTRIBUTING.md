# Contributing

Thank you for helping us improve our games, our website and our policies. Our
game and website code is private, and we don't take pull requests from outside
the studio, so tell us what you found instead. We're a small studio and answer
as soon as we can.

## Report something

Search the
[existing issues](https://github.com/Severed-Signal-Studios/.github/issues?q=is%3Aissue)
first, in case someone has already reported it. Then
[open an issue](https://github.com/Severed-Signal-Studios/.github/issues/new/choose)
and pick the form that fits:

- **Bug report** for something in one of our games that doesn't work as it
  should
- **Accessibility barrier** for anything that makes one of our games or our
  website hard or impossible to use
- **Feedback or idea** for everything else

If you don't have a GitHub account, report a bug or barrier by email to
[support@severedsignal.com](mailto:support@severedsignal.com) or on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/nz9bkGb2Fv), and we add it to the issues without
your personal details.

Issues and their attachments are public. The
[support guide](https://github.com/Severed-Signal-Studios/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md)
says what to include and what to check before you attach a file.

## Typos and mistakes

If you spot a typo, a broken link or something out of date in our policies,
issue forms or website, tell us with the feedback form, by email or on Discord.
A mistake in a game is a bug, so use the bug report form.

## Ideas

We read every piece of feedback, though we can't promise to act on all of it.
By sharing an idea, you agree we may use it in our games without owing you
payment or credit.

## Other policies

- **Security problems:** report them privately, as the
  [security policy](https://github.com/Severed-Signal-Studios/.github/security/policy)
  explains. Don't open an issue.
- **Accessibility:** the
  [accessibility statement](https://github.com/Severed-Signal-Studios/.github/blob/main/ACCESSIBILITY.md)
  says what we aim for and what happens after you report a barrier.
- **Conduct:** our
  [code of conduct](https://github.com/Severed-Signal-Studios/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
  applies in issues, comments and our other spaces, and says how to report a
  concern.
