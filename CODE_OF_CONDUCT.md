# Code of conduct

Severed Signal Studios follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/)
in its community spaces.

## Where it applies

- Issues, comments and discussions in the
  [Severed Signal Studios](https://github.com/Severed-Signal-Studios) GitHub
  organization
- Our [Discord](https://discord.gg/nz9bkGb2Fv) server
- Any other community space we run
- Anywhere someone represents the studio in public

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, email
[support@severedsignal.com](mailto:support@severedsignal.com) and start the subject
line with "Conduct". On Discord, you can also message a moderator.

We read every report. We don't share who made it without their permission,
unless the law requires us to.

If the behavior is by someone at the studio, you can also report it to the
platform where it happened or, at an event, to its organizers. They act under
their own rules.

## Enforcement

We follow the Contributor Covenant's guidance on
[addressing and repairing harm](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/#addressing-and-repairing-harm).
Depending on what happened, we may warn someone privately, hide or remove
comments, lock conversations, or block someone from our spaces, either for a
while or for good.
